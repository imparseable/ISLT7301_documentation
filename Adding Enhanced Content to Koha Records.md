# Adding Enhanced Content to Your Koha Records

*You will need to have fully set up your initial Koha build for this and created at least one item in your catalog*

This guide is to show you how to add Enhanced Content so your book submissions include an image without you having to import one. 

1. First you will need to click into the Koha Administration section. 
<img width="2525" height="556" alt="Kelley_koha_admin" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e5b86f3b-d4b5-462f-978e-f420ac14fbff" />

2. From here you need to find the global settings. You can just type in global in the search bar of system preferences to be brought to the correct page. 
<img width="2307" height="762" alt="Kelley_koha_global_search" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b5756082-2dde-4fa2-b2a7-4cafcf6d161a" />

3. You'll need to select Enhanced content from the left sidebar. 
<img width="2522" height="985" alt="Kelley_koha_enhanced_content" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a1faf243-ff69-46cd-b904-715725e319e2" />

4. There are a ton of options here for Enhanced content. I decided to go with GoogleJackets. In the original documentation that I read to find how to add this, it said do NOT enable more than one option at a time, as it tends to glitch the system on what information to include.
<img width="2520" height="821" alt="Kelley_koha_GoogleJackets" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a8676f87-1cee-43a7-af1f-71f35b538ebb" />

5. Now on your patron side of your Koha install, your book should include an image provided by GoogleJackets!
<img width="1877" height="752" alt="Kelley_koha_item_record" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cf6bb5e4-2605-4c93-8066-f2aff22897cb" />
